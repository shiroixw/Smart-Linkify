v1.2: a rebuilt link engine and tighter matching

Oct 12, 2026 · 6 min read

This release rebuilds the link detection engine, covering more real-world formats while cutting false positives. Start with the Manifest V3 section of https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions if you are porting an extension.

For regex boundary issues, the grouping and assertion chapters in https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Guide/Regular_expressions are worth a read; the performance numbers live at https://web.dev/articles/vitals .

To upgrade, open chrome://extensions and hit “Reload”. Older builds are archived at https://github.com/xiaowulang-turbo/Smart-Linkify/releases .

Note: the stricter matching only applies when “strict mode” is on. Regression tests cover 23 cases.
